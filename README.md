# BD-Road-Accident

Exploratory data analysis of road accidents in Bangladesh, done in a Jupyter Notebook.

## Overview

Road accidents are one of the leading causes of death and injury in Bangladesh. This project uses accident data to look for patterns in when, where and why accidents happen, and who is most affected.

## Goals

- Clean and prepare the road accident dataset
- Explore trends over time (yearly, monthly, by day of week and time of day)
- Compare accidents, deaths and injuries across divisions and districts
- Look at the vehicle types, road types and causes involved
- Visualize the findings with clear charts
- Summarize key insights that could inform road safety efforts
